Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Information we process

MoneyMate processes your connected account email, profile details you provide, transactions, categories, people and shared balances, and preferences. Uploaded statements are processed to prepare transactions for review and import.

Google and Gmail access

Connecting Gmail requires your permission through Google OAuth. MoneyMate reads supported bank alert emails to extract transaction information. Authorization tokens are stored to allow future syncs. The app does not need your Google password. Reading this policy or the Terms of Service requires no login or Google authorization.

Use and storage

Information is used to display your ledger, calculate balances, provide spending summaries, and sync bank alerts. Records are stored in the database used by this installation: on the computer running a local installation, or in the configured database for a hosted installation. Statement previews are temporarily held in server memory; imported transactions remain in the database.

Providers and cookies

Google processes authentication and Gmail API requests. Hosted installations also rely on their hosting and database providers. MoneyMate uses session and OAuth state cookies for authentication security. The main app loads icon fonts from Google Fonts.

Your choices and retention

You can disconnect Gmail in the app or revoke access through your Google Account. Disconnecting does not erase previously imported records. You can delete transactions in the app; identifiers may be retained to prevent deleted entries from being imported again. For account or database deletion requests, contact the operator who provided your MoneyMate installation.

Updates and questions

Policy updates will appear on this page. For questions about information handled by your installation, contact the operator who provided you access.
